2. Getting Dressed

2a. The Romantic Look

You pick the flowy floral dress with the puffy sleeves. You add some gold hoops, a little blush, and let your hair fall in loose waves.

You feel soft and kind of dreamy, like the main character in a movie.

Go check the mirror

2b. The Casual Look

You go with your favorite baggy jeans, a plain white tee, and your sneakers that are a little dirty but broken in just right. Hair goes up in a claw clip. Barely any makeup.

It's comfy. It's easy. It's you on a normal day.

Go check the mirror

2c. The Professional Look

You put on the black blazer, tailored trousers, and the loafers you only wear for important things. Slicked back bun, a watch, and a neutral lip.

You look like someone who has their life together, even if you don't totally feel like it.

Go check the mirror
