3. The Mirror

You stand in front of the mirror and turn to the left. Then to the right. Then you step back and look again.

Do I like this?

Part of you thinks it looks great. Another part of you is already wondering what everyone else is going to think.

The clock is ticking. You have to decide.

4a. Change It

Something feels off. You pull it off and toss it back on the bed.

Change outfit (back to the closet)

4b. Keep It

You take a deep breath. This is the one. You grab your bag and your keys.

Keep outfit and head out
