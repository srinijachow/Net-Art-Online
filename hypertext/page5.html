6. How It Ends

6a. Confident

You stand up a little straighter. You chose this, and you like it. Someone comes up and tells you they love your outfit, and you smile and say thanks like it's no big deal (it's kind of a big deal).

The rest of the day you barely think about what you're wearing. You're too busy just being yourself.

6b. Insecure

You keep pulling at your sleeve and fixing your hair. You notice what everyone else is wearing and start comparing. Maybe you should have picked something else.

But then you realize nobody is really looking at you as much as you think. They're all worried about their own outfits too.

Clothes are more than an outfit

Either way, what you put on this morning changed how you walked into the room, how you talked to people, and how you thought they saw you.

It was never really just about the clothes.

Start over and pick a different outfit
