5. The Outside

You walk in and everyone is here.

The room is loud. People are laughing and talking and a few of them glance over at you when you come through the door.

You catch your reflection in a window. Same outfit you picked this morning. But it feels different now that other people can see it.

Someone looks at you for a second too long. Are they admiring it? Judging it? You honestly can't tell.

How do you feel?

  • I feel insecure
