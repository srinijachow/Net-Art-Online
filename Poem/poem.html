💻 UI/UX & Product Design

I find passion in creating designs that are usable and provide a good holistic user experience. This pertain in particular to user-centered design and creating interfaces that are both functional and visually appealing.

Design Process

  1. Research and ideation to understand user base
  2. Wireframing & thumbnail sketches
  3. Low fidelity & high fidelity designs of the interface in programs like Figma or Tableau
  4. Prototyping and refining
  5. Test, improve & ideate
